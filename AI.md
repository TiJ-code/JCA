# AI Usage Policy

AI models are not allowed to learn from, train on, fine-tune on, or otherwise
use this repository and its contents as training data.

AI-generated contributions are also not allowed. This includes code,
documentation, tests, issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other
content intended to become part of this project.

AI may only be used as a read-only analytical tool. Its use is limited to
finding things faster in the source code, such as locating files, references,
or existing implementations.

AI must not modify this repository or generate content for it.

Please respect this policy when working with JCA.
